# Sum up 23 November's world news in one image

Entry for the make.fun bounty [Sum up 23 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261123).

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the bundled
`entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`. The program uses no network and
produces identical bytes on every run.

## The image

A newspaper front-page style collage of Monday 23 November 2026:

- Backlash in Antalya as the COP31 climate talks collapse with no deal
- A Russian strike near Kyiv kills at least 10
- Tokyo's Nikkei falls about 5% as an AI sell-off spreads across Asia
- Denmark foils an alleged plot against a concert in Copenhagen
- Drone strikes hit homes and a UN building in Khartoum, Sudan
- US Democrats clinch their biggest House majority since 1992

Ticker: Mexico captures an FBI most-wanted cartel boss; Marc Marquez
wins his eighth MotoGP title; gold jumps as investors seek safety.

Image: PNG, 1024 x 1024, under 1 MiB.
